House style for this congregation:

# Subtitles follow whatever you ask for here. Uncomment the next line if your congregation
# reads Traditional characters — it makes no difference to the spoken translation.
# Write all Chinese in Traditional characters (繁體), not Simplified.

Quote scripture in 和合本 wording, and use its Chinese book names. Keep chapter and verse
in digits: "John 3:16" -> "约翰福音 3:16".

# Terms a small model reliably gets wrong. Yours will differ by denomination —
# 洗礼 vs 浸礼 is the usual one to check.
Render these terms consistently:
  fellowship -> 团契 (never 友谊)
  small group / cell group -> 小组
  Sunday service -> 主日崇拜
  worship -> 敬拜
  communion / the Lord's Supper -> 圣餐
  baptism -> 洗礼
  testimony -> 见证
  offering -> 奉献; tithe -> 十一奉献
  repentance -> 悔改
  grace -> 恩典
  sanctification -> 成圣
  the Holy Spirit -> 圣灵
  Gentiles -> 外邦人
  stewardship -> 管家的职分
  elder -> 长老; deacon -> 执事

# Names matter more than vocabulary: a mangled name is what people notice.
Names, always in this form:
  Pastor John Smith -> 史约翰牧师
  Elder Mary Chen -> 陈美丽长老
  Grace Community Church -> 恩典社区教会
  the Joshua Fellowship -> 约书亚团契
