Copy this to glossary.txt and replace the placeholders with your own. Every line here is
appended to the translator's prompt on every single utterance, so it costs latency: keep it
to the terms your congregation would notice getting wrong, not a dictionary. Thirty lines is
plenty; two hundred will slow the translation down measurably.

House style for this congregation:

Quote scripture in 和合本 wording, and use its Chinese book names. Keep chapter and verse
in digits: "John 3:16" -> "约翰福音 3:16".

Render these terms consistently:
  fellowship -> 团契 (never 友谊)
  small group / cell group -> 小组
  Sunday service -> 主日崇拜
  worship -> 敬拜
  communion / the Lord's Supper -> 圣餐
  baptism -> 洗礼
  testimony -> 见证
  offering -> 奉献; tithe -> 十一奉献
  repentance -> 悔改
  grace -> 恩典
  sanctification -> 成圣
  the Holy Spirit -> 圣灵
  Gentiles -> 外邦人
  stewardship -> 管家的职分
  elder -> 长老; deacon -> 执事

Names, always in this form:
  Pastor John Smith -> 史约翰牧师
  Elder Mary Chen -> 陈美丽长老
  Grace Community Church -> 恩典社区教会
  the Joshua Fellowship -> 约书亚团契
